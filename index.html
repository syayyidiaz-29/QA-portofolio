03 — Process

My Testing Approach

01
Understand the App

Review requirements, user flows, and feature specs before writing a single test case. Context-first testing catches more bugs.

02
Design Test Scenarios

Build test cases covering both expected behaviors and edge cases — including boundary conditions and negative scenarios.

03
Execute & Document

Run tests methodically and document every bug with clear reproduction steps, expected vs. actual results, and severity ratings.

04
Verify & Report

Retest after fixes, confirm resolutions, and deliver structured reports that developers and stakeholders can actually act on.
